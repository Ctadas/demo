Online Registration — 4 Steps

Fully online e-filing, with certificates issued in as fast as 2-3 business days. A dedicated consultant guides you at every step — you provide the documents, we handle the rest.

STEP 01

Consultation & Plan Confirmation

Day 1

Business Background & Plan

Understand your business background and registration purpose, then tailor a plan to fit.

Choose Company Type

Private Limited Company (most common) or Branch Office.

Shareholding Structure

1-50 shareholders, shareholding ratios, and nominee arrangements.

Select Company Name

Provide 2-3 preferred names; Chinese names end with "有限公司", English names end with "Limited".

Confirm Registered Capital

Default HK$10,000, no capital verification required (excess subject to government stamp duty).

Choose Service Package

One-stop plan: Registration + Secretary + Address + Bank Account Opening.

Tip: Company name search is the first step — we complete the availability check right away and switch to a backup name if it is taken.
STEP 02

Client Document Preparation

Day 1-2

Identity Documents

Passport is sufficient — plus a handheld ID photo of each shareholder & director.

Proof of Address

Utility bill / bank statement / credit card statement within the last 3 months — documents in your country are accepted; non-English ones need a translation.

Company Information

Company name, business nature, registered capital & shareholding ratio.

KYC Due Diligence Form

Source of funds declaration, tax residency declaration, and more.

Tip: Your home-country ID and address documents are accepted — just make sure non-English documents include an English translation.
STEP 03

Document Signing & Submission

Day 1-2

Articles of Association

Prepared bilingually for your review and confirmation.

NNC1 Incorporation Form

The incorporation form, drafted on your behalf.

Documents to Sign

Articles, NNC1, share certificates, director resolutions, first director notice, and more.

Signing Method

E-signature (with digital certificate verification) or paper signing returned by mail.

Submit to Business Registration Office

The Business Registration Certificate (BR) application is submitted at the same time.

Tip: Online e-filing means no visit to Hong Kong — every document can be signed online. For urgent cases, a 24-hour express registration service is available.

Client Document Checklist

Everything you need to prepare, at a glance.

Identity Documents

  • Passport bio page (sufficient)
  • Handheld ID photo (each shareholder & director)
  • National ID card (optional, if available)
  • If shareholder is a company: business licence + Articles

Proof of Address

  • Utility bill within the last 3 months
  • Or bank statement within the last 3 months
  • Or credit card statement (with address)
  • Must clearly show your name + residential address
  • Documents from your country are accepted; non-English needs a translation

Company Information

  • Proposed company name (Chinese & English)
  • Registered capital amount
  • Shareholders & shareholding ratio
  • Director appointment details
  • Business nature description (Chinese & English)

Compliance Documents

  • KYC due diligence form (template provided)
  • Tax residency self-declaration
  • Source of funds declaration
  • Brief business plan
