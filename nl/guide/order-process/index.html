From Your Store to Their Doorstep

When a customer places an order on your Shopify store, our system kicks into action. Here is every step we take — from order sync to final delivery.

1. Order Sync

The moment an order is placed in your Shopify store, it is automatically synced to our fulfillment system — no manual forwarding needed.

2. SKU Conversion & Pricing

If it's a new product — one we haven't quoted before — our team converts it to a local SKU and fills in the pricing. Products with an existing SKU skip this step and proceed instantly.

3. Daily Invoicing

Every working day (09:00–19:30 China time), our team generates invoices for all orders received during that Chinese calendar day. Once the invoice is paid, the procurement process begins.

4. Procurement

Invoices are handed to our purchasing department, who source the products from our trusted network of factories.

5. Production & Inbound

Factories produce and ship the goods to our warehouse. Once received, the items enter our inspection queue.

6. Quality Control

Every item undergoes a thorough quality check — we verify product condition, quantity, and packaging before release.

7. Ship Out

Once QC is passed, your order is packed and shipped to your customer with a tracking number — completing the journey from your store to their doorstep.
