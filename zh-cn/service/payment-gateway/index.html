Your Part — 5 Steps

This is everything you need to do. Each step is quick — and your dedicated consultant guides you through it.

STEP 01

Submit Your Company Documents

Day 1

Certificate of Incorporation (CI)

The official certificate confirming your company is registered.

Business Registration Certificate (BR)

Your company's business licence — the latest annual version is required.

NNC1 or NAR1

NNC1 if your company is under 1 year old; NAR1 Annual Return if it is older.

Company Seal

Used for signing the agreement and ownership documents.

Good news: These are the same documents you received at registration — nothing new to prepare. If your company name has changed, the NNC2 notice is also required.
STEP 02

Identity & Ownership Documents

Day 1-2

Director's ID

Passport (bio page + signature page) or ID card — both sides on one page, signed.

Ultimate Beneficial Owner (UBO) ID

Valid passport or ID card for every owner holding more than 25% of the company.

Ownership Structure Chart

Shows the real holding structure up to the natural-person owner, signed by a director and stamped.

Merchant Questionnaire

A short KYC questionnaire (MAF form) about your business — a template is provided.

Tip: At least one director's identification is required; if your application is flagged for enhanced due diligence (EDD), identification for two directors is needed. We guide you through this if it applies.
STEP 03

Address & Bank Account Proof

Day 1-3

Company Address Proof

Lease contract, utility invoice, or one of the alternative options — your consultant explains the choices.

Director & UBO Address Proof

Utility bill, bank statement or credit card statement within the last 3 months.

HK Company Bank Account

A physical bank account in your company's name — account opening letter or a recent monthly statement.

Settlement Details

Your bank logo, company name, account number and currency must be clearly visible.

Tip: Your home-country utility or bank documents are accepted for address proof — just make sure non-English documents include an English translation. If you have not opened your Hong Kong company bank account yet, we can assist — settlement funds are paid into this account.
STEP 04

Website Details & Transaction History

Day 1-3

6-Month Transaction Data

Monthly volume, average order value, refunds and chargebacks from your current payment provider.

New Store? No Problem

If you have no transaction history yet, your website promotion plan is accepted instead.

Website Details

Your store URL, platform (Shopify or other), and the products or services you sell.

Risk Profile

Your history helps the bank understand your business and set the right risk level.

Tip: A clear transaction history speeds up approval. If your website is brand new, a simple promotion plan — channels, budget and expected sales — is all you need.
STEP 05

Sign the Service Agreement

Day 2-4

Service Agreement

Signed and stamped by the company, with director signature and company seal.

Service Fees

Setup and annual service fees confirmed — details are shared with you directly.

Risk Control Rules

Fraud-prevention rules tailored to your business, balancing approval rates and safety.

Dedicated Support Assigned

A 1-on-1 account manager and technical support are assigned to your project.

Tip: Your account manager stays with you after go-live — settlement queries, risk questions and rate reviews all go through one dedicated contact.
