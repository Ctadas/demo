View daily commissions from your students. Use the filters to search by student or date range, and track quantities and commissions at a glance.

Each entry shows the Date Time, Customer (student name), Quantity (fulfilled orders), and Commission amount. You can also see the Total commission at the top right.

03 Per-Order Records & Cash Out

See every order placed by your students and the commission earned for each. The total commission is displayed at the top, and you can request a Cash Out directly from this page.

View all your past withdrawal requests — account, amount, date, and status at a glance. Each record shows whether the withdrawal has been processed successfully.
